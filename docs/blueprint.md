# **App Name**: AFCII Connect

## Core Features:

- Page Content Display: Render all static and rich text content for informational pages such as Accueil, À propos, Nos pôles, Rejoindre l’AFCII, and Partenaires.
- Dynamic Projects Listing: Display current and past projects with their detailed descriptions, goals, and outcomes.
- News & Events Section: Provide a dynamic feed for news articles, organizational announcements, seminars, and conferences, with basic management capabilities for administrators.
- Contact Form & Inquiry Management: Enable visitors to send messages and inquiries through a user-friendly contact form, with backend storage and retrieval for AFCII staff.
- Multi-language Support (French & English): Enable users to switch between French and English content across the entire website for broader accessibility.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color, a vibrant green, symbolizes growth, sustainability, and vitality, reflecting the center's focus on development and innovation. It establishes a fresh and energetic presence.
- A clean white is used as the main background color, ensuring maximum readability and a spacious, modern feel, allowing content to stand out.
- An accent color of deep dark green is used for important calls to action, headings, and key interactive elements, providing a strong contrast and reinforcing the organic, natural themes while maintaining professionalism.
- For headlines, 'Alegreya' (serif) brings an intellectual and elegant touch, suitable for a center focused on research and education. For body text, 'Inter' (sans-serif) is chosen for its exceptional readability, modern clarity, and neutrality, ensuring long-form content is accessible and engaging.
- Utilize minimalist, modern line-art icons that communicate ideas clearly and concisely, aligning with a forward-thinking and sophisticated brand image. Icons should be easily understandable and contribute to intuitive navigation.
- Implement a clean, spacious, and responsive layout that prioritizes content legibility and ease of navigation across all devices. Emphasize well-structured sections and clear content hierarchy to guide the user's attention.
- Incorporate subtle and purposeful animations for transitions between sections, hover states on interactive elements, and loading indicators. These animations should enhance the user experience by providing fluid feedback without being distracting.
